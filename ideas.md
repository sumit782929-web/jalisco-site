# Jalisco Website Design Brief

## Stylistic Approaches

### Theme Name: Marigold Editorial
Very warm, editorial restaurant branding with a cream paper foundation, ink-like typography, and citrus accents. It would feel intimate, considered, and premium.

**Probability:** 0.06

### Theme Name: Midnight Cantina
A dark, atmospheric dining identity with saturated colour blocks, vivid signage-inspired accents, and a late-night energy.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Modern Fiesta
A disciplined pop-art system that pairs gallery-like whitespace with a few bold colour punches, expressive type, playful graphic marks, and polished restaurant photography.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen Approach: Modern Fiesta

### Design Movement
Contemporary pop art with editorial restaurant art direction and restrained Swiss-style structure. The composition is intentionally asymmetric, giving the brand a confident point of view without turning the page into visual noise.

### Core Principles
1. Keep the information architecture calm and minimal so the restaurant remains the focus.
2. Use bold colour as punctuation: terracotta, avocado, mustard, and cobalt appear in deliberate blocks, tags, and graphic accents.
3. Combine oversized expressive display type with highly legible utility text.
4. Make every primary action obvious: explore the menu, reserve a table, call, WhatsApp, and find the restaurant.

### Color Philosophy
A warm bone-white background makes the site feel tactile and food-friendly. Deep espresso provides sophistication and strong contrast. Jalisco orange-terracotta is the ownable signature, while avocado, mustard, and cobalt supply controlled pop-art energy. Colour should guide attention and evoke a lively table—not decorate every surface.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an editorial, offset composition rather than a centered marketing grid. Sections alternate between wide photographic crops, offset text rails, split colour panels, and narrow information bands. The homepage should feel like a designed poster that remains easy to scan.

### Signature Elements
1. A small circular sunburst mark and hand-drawn star details used as recurring brand punctuation.
2. Slightly rotated menu tags and photographic frames that feel like curated print ephemera.
3. A strong orange-red action button that consistently means “come to the table.”

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel tactile and direct. Buttons compress slightly on press, image cards lift or shift by a few pixels on hover, menu filters change instantly, and mobile actions stay within easy thumb reach. No interaction should compete with the food photography or make the website feel like an app dashboard.

### Animation
Use short, snappy transitions under 300ms with transform and opacity only. Stagger the first screen’s visual elements gently, allow the hero image to settle into place, and use reduced-motion fallbacks. Hover states should be noticeable but restrained: a slight frame offset, colour shift, or underline reveal is enough.

### Typography System
Use **Bodoni Moda** for expressive display headlines and **DM Sans** for navigation, descriptions, labels, and form controls. Headlines should use large, tight, high-contrast serif sizing; body copy should stay compact and relaxed. Uppercase micro-labels should be letter-spaced and used sparingly.

### Brand Essence
**Jalisco is a bold, contemporary dining destination in Noida for people who want memorable food, atmosphere, and a place worth sharing.**

Personality adjectives: **Lively, assured, generous.**

### Brand Voice
Headlines should be short, sensory, and confident. Calls to action should sound like invitations, not software prompts. Microcopy should be warm, specific, and useful.

Example lines:
- “Come hungry. Leave with a story.”
- “Make tonight the good kind of loud.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a custom wordmark with a high-contrast serif “J” that carries a small sunburst cut into its terminal, paired with a compact circular sunburst icon for favicon and mobile use. The mark should be usable in one colour and as a terracotta-on-bone lockup.

### Signature Brand Color
**Jalisco Terracotta — #E94F32.** It owns the primary action language, adds appetite and energy, and remains distinctive against the warm bone-white foundation.

## Implementation Reminder

Every CSS and component file should preserve the Modern Fiesta direction: calm editorial structure, warm bone-white base, espresso text, Jalisco Terracotta actions, expressive serif headlines, and restrained pop-art accents.
